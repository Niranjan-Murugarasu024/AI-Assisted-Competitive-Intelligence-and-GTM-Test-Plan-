Growth Architecture

Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

Strategic positioning framework designed to capture skeptical high-intent learners by providing proof of capability prior to commercial transaction.

1. Proposed Value Proposition & Positioning

"Don't spend ₹3,00,000 on an AI course until you prove you can build the first vertical slice in 5 hours for free."

Commercial platforms sell the dream of a ₹30 LPA salary while disclaiming all legal placement responsibility. Proof-Before-Purchase shifts the dynamic from high-pressure blind trust to evidence-based learner autonomy.

2. Target Customer Journey & Funnel

Stage 1: Doubt Trigger
Learner is shocked by unadvertised GST or pushy sales calls; searches for unbiased program comparisons.
Stage 2: Diagnostic Fit
Takes 3-minute local diagnostic to calibrate real weekly time budget and receive a scoped micro-project.
Stage 3: Proof-of-Work
Builds micro-project vertical slice and grades against objective 3-tier rubric.
Stage 4: Informed Choice
Uses the 5 Provider Questions to challenge sales reps and make a defensible investment decision.

3. Measurable Funnel KPIs & Guardrails

Primary Funnel KPI
Qualified Consultation / Decision Readiness Rate: Ratio of diagnostic completers who download the audit workbook and report clear purchase clarity.
Quality & Trust Guardrail
Zero Commercial Kickbacks: No hidden affiliate bounty deals with bootcamps; all comparison data remains strictly auditable and transparent.
Validation Protocol

Five-Experiment GTM Planner

Structured validation experiments designed to test customer problem resonance, prototype usability, and messaging traction. No fabricated metrics; all experiments are marked "Proposed" until executed with live participants.

Audit Rigor

Research Methodology & Limitations

Standards of evidence, primary source verification protocols, and transparent identification of market unknowns.

1. Primary Source Extraction & Normalization

Data was extracted directly from official program landing pages, partner university continuing education portals, and published fee schedules as of 2026-10-22. Where net prices were quoted without tax, 18% Indian GST was calculated and displayed explicitly to reflect total out-of-pocket costs.

Marketing testimonials and self-reported salary hike figures were categorized as "Marketing Claims with Legal Caveats" and isolated from confirmed programmatic attributes.

2. Four-Tier Epistemic Classification

Observed Fact
Directly cited from legal terms, public URLs, or institutional fee tables.
Inferred Pattern
Logical deduction derived from multiple aligned data points (e.g., GST exclusion pattern).
Working Hypothesis
Proposed customer behavior pattern awaiting empirical test confirmation.
Unknown / Unverified
Proprietary metrics (e.g. true cohort drop-out rates) that cannot be verified without insider audit.

3. Known Limitations

  • Commercial providers frequently adjust tuition and promotional discount structures on short notice.
  • Admissions criteria on websites represent minimum prerequisites; actual cohort admission standards may vary.
  • Placement assistance efficacy varies dramatically by prior candidate background and macroeconomic tech hiring cycles.
