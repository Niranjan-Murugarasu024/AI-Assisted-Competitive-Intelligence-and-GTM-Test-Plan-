# Research Brief: Proof Before Purchase — AI & Data Upskilling Market Analysis (2026)

## Executive Summary
In 2026, the market for executive and professional education in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science across India and emerging tech hubs has matured into an intensive, high-ticket consumer sector. Programs offered by commercial aggregators (upGrad, Simplilearn, Great Learning, Emeritus) in collaboration with prestigious academic institutions (IIIT Bangalore, Purdue University, UT Austin McCombs, IIT Delhi, ISI Kolkata) command tuition fees ranging from **₹2,30,100 to ₹4,70,000 INR**.

However, prospective students face severe information asymmetries:
1. **Unadvertised 18% GST surcharges** adding ₹35,000 to ₹54,000 at checkout.
2. **Ambiguous credential claims**, where executive continuing education certificates are marketed with regular university degree logos.
3. **Disclaimed placement guarantees**, where high-pressure sales calls promise career transformation while legal contracts disclaim employment outcomes.
4. **Shallow project depth**, where "25+ projects" frequently consist of pre-filled 45-minute Jupyter notebook walkthroughs.

This research brief establishes the business justification for **Proof Before Purchase**—an interactive, deterministic role-fit diagnostic and micro-project evaluation experience designed to de-risk high-stakes education decisions for prospective learners.

---

## 1. Business Problem & Target Audience

### 1.1 The Buyer Dilemma
Prospective learners typically fall into three primary cohorts:
- **Non-Technical Career Switchers (0-3 years exp):** Professionals in operations, sales, or non-STEM fields hoping to enter the data domain. They possess minimal programming background and are vulnerable to unrealistic placement claims.
- **Adjacent Technical Practitioners (2-6 years exp):** Traditional software developers, QA engineers, or business intelligence analysts seeking to modernize their skillsets with Generative AI and ML. Their primary risk is curriculum superficiality.
- **Mid-to-Senior Engineering Leads (7+ years exp):** Managers looking for architectural fluency and technical leadership credentials. Their primary risk is time commitment overload.

### 1.2 Core Friction Points
- **Financial Risk:** Committing 3 to 6 months of post-tax salary to an upfront, non-refundable tuition payment.
- **Cognitive Uncertainty:** Not knowing whether one has the prerequisite aptitude for linear algebra, probabilistic programming, or cloud deployments until weeks into the paid syllabus.
- **Aggressive Sales Funnels:** Immediate phone calls from admissions telemarketers offering artificial "limited-time scholarship discounts" if payment is executed within 24 hours.

## 3. The Proof Before Purchase Solution
Instead of relying on marketing brochures, Proof Before Purchase provides:
1. **Deterministic Role-Fit Diagnostic:** Calibrates role suitability based on existing skills, targeted gaps, and realistic weekly study budgets.
2. **Standardized Micro-Project:** A concrete, 3 to 10 hour vertical slice using open-source tools and real datasets.
3. **Objective Evaluation Rubric:** A 3-tier grading rubric (Beginner, Proficient, Production-Ready) across Data Hygiene, Methodological Rigor, and Communication.
4. **Admissions Due-Diligence Checklist:** Five targeted questions to ask admissions representatives before signing a financing agreement.
