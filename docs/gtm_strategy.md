# Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy & Positioning Framework (2026)

## 1. Executive Positioning Statement
For working technical professionals and ambitious career switchers evaluating ₹2.5L+ AI and data upskilling programs who are skeptical of aggressive sales pitches, **Proof Before Purchase** is an evidence-based role-fit diagnostic that enables prospective learners to test their real-world project capability and uncover hidden program caveats before committing their money.

Unlike commercial edtech comparison portals that monetize through affiliate enrollment bounties, Proof Before Purchase is a 100% transparent, local-first consumer advocacy tool that provides objective rubrics, workload feasibility checks, and admissions diligence checklists.

---

## 2. Target Market Segments

### Primary Segment: The Disillusioned Evaluator
- **Demographics:** Working professionals (2-7 years experience), current salary ₹6 LPA - ₹18 LPA.
- **Trigger Event:** Attended an edtech "free masterclass", downloaded a syllabus, and received 4 phone calls from aggressive sales telemarketers within 48 hours.
- **Pain Point:** Felt pressured to pay a non-refundable deposit before knowing if they can handle the coding or math workload.
- **Job-to-be-Done:** "Help me objectively test if I can actually do this work before I risk 3 months of salary."

### Secondary Segment: The Technical Modernizer
- **Demographics:** Senior Software Engineers / QA / Database Admins (7+ years experience).
- **Trigger Event:** Mandate to integrate Generative AI / LLMs into production applications.
- **Pain Point:** Fear that commercial programs will teach elementary syntax and pre-baked notebooks rather than production engineering.
- **Job-to-be-Done:** "Show me the real technical depth of each program and provide an advanced architecture challenge to test my gaps."

## 4. Primary KPI & Safety Guardrails

### Primary Metric: Qualified Decision Readiness Rate (QDRR)
Defined as:
$$\text{QDRR} = \frac{\text{Users who complete diagnostic and download the Audit Package}}{\text{Total unique landing page sessions}} \times 100$$
- **Target Benchmark:** $\ge 8.5\%$

### Guardrails
1. **Zero Lead Selling:** User responses are processed 100% locally in-memory. No phone numbers or emails are captured or sold to third-party bootcamps.
2. **Neutrality Audit:** Any sponsored listing or affiliate relationship is strictly prohibited. All data reflects verified facts.
3. **No Employment Guarantees:** Output explicitly disclaims career placement promises, reinforcing its role as an advisory diagnostic tool.
