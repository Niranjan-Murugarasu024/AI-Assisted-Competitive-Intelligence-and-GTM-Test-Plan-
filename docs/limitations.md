# Research Limitations, Comparability Constraints & Epistemic Boundaries

## 1. Dynamic Pricing & Unadvertised Promotional Discounts
- **Observed Challenge:** Commercial edtech vendors employ dynamic pricing algorithms and frequent "scholarship discounts" negotiated individually by admissions telemarketers.
- **Epistemic Constraint:** The tuition figures documented in this benchmark represent publicly verifiable base rates as of October 2026. A student negotiating directly on the phone may receive an ad-hoc concession of 10% to 25%, often tied to immediate payment deadlines.
- **Handling:** We explicitly record the published baseline and note financing/tax caveats rather than reporting anecdotal negotiated discounts.

---

## 2. Continuing Education vs. Formal Academic Diplomas
- **Observed Challenge:** Program titles like "Post Graduate Program" (PGP) are not legally equivalent to statutory academic degrees.
- **Epistemic Constraint:** Four out of five audited programs award institutional certificates issued by executive continuing education departments (e.g. IIT Delhi CEP, UT Austin McCombs Executive Education, Purdue Online), while only one (ISI Kolkata) awards an academic postgraduate diploma recognized by higher education regulatory bodies.
- **Handling:** We categorize credential types explicitly into "Executive Education Certificate", "Bootcamp PG Certificate", "Executive PG Diploma", and "Academic Postgraduate Diploma" with clear disclaimer notices.

---

## 3. Placement & Career Support Claim Opacity
- **Observed Challenge:** High-pressure marketing highlights placement metrics (e.g., "55% average salary hike", "3,000+ hiring partners").
- **Epistemic Constraint:** These metrics are self-reported aggregates not audited by any independent third party. They frequently include candidates who were already employed in data/AI roles and received standard internal corporate increments.
- **Handling:** Every career support entry includes the legally binding disclaimer found in the provider's terms of service. We classify these figures as "Marketing Claims with Legal Caveats" rather than independently verified outcomes.

---

## 4. Diagnostic Advisory Scope
- **Observed Challenge:** Users might interpret diagnostic output as a formal certification of career readiness.
- **Epistemic Constraint:** The role-fit diagnostic is an advisory educational self-assessment tool. It evaluates alignment with self-reported skill levels, time budgets, and target roles using deterministic rule templates.
- **Handling:** Every diagnostic output displays a clear legal disclaimer clarifying that the tool does not guarantee academic admission, employment, or corporate hiring outcomes.
